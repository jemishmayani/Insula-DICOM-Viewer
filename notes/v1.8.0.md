Free, open-source DICOM viewer for Android 7.0 and newer. Your images stay on your phone.

> **Not a medical device.** For reference, teaching, and patient use, not primary diagnosis. See [Intended use and limitations](https://github.com/jemishmayani/Insula-DICOM-Viewer/blob/main/docs/INTENDED_USE.md).

### New in 1.8.0: 3D VRT
Open it with **3D** in the viewer's quick bar.

- **Rendered on your phone's GPU** (OpenGL ES 3.0). Phones without it get a slower basic mode; quality is chosen for your phone and shown on screen.
- **Automatic tissue separation for contrast CT** such as coronary CTA: bone, vessels and heart chambers, calcium, soft tissue, lungs, and skin, from thresholds measured on each scan. Vessels touching bone stay vessels.
- **Fine-tune it yourself:** per-tissue colour, opacity, and density range; tap a structure to hide it, keep only it, or move it to another class; draw to cut; clip; undo.
- **Presets** for coronary CTA, vessels, bones, all tissues, lungs, vessel MIP, and skin; VRT, MIP, MinIP, and Surface modes; lighting; standard views; spin.
- **Sessions saved inside the study**, including your cuts, so you can continue later, and they travel with study-set exports.
- **Captures:** save or share an image, add it to the study, or add a 36-view rotation series.

Automatic separation distinguishes tissue types, not individual organs, and can be wrong on real scans: check the result and correct it with Pick and Cut. The in-app help and Guide explain every tool and what the quality levels mean.

### Install
1. Download **InsulaDICOMViewer-v1.8.0.apk** below on your Android phone.
2. Open it, and allow installing from this source when Android asks.
3. Already using Insula? Install over it (or use **About › Check for updates**); your studies and settings are kept.
