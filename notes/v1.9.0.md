Free, open-source DICOM viewer for Android 7.0 and newer. Your images stay on your phone.

> **Not a medical device.** For reference, teaching, and patient use, not primary diagnosis. See [Intended use and limitations](https://github.com/jemishmayani/Insula-DICOM-Viewer/blob/main/docs/INTENDED_USE.md).

### What's new in 1.9.0
- **Smart tools for the study type:** Insula recognizes spine, CVJ, brain, cardiac CTA, CTA, CTPA, trauma, neck, chest, and abdomen studies. The **Smart** button offers suited window presets, MPR planes, MIP and MinIP slabs, 3D, and measurement shortcuts (ADI, BDI, Chamberlain, McGregor, canal diameter, Cobb, midline shift, hematoma volume) with commonly cited reference values, for guidance only.
- **New measurements:** point to line, and hematoma volume (ABC/2). The result bar updates as you edit.
- **Window presets** appear with the brightness tool, in the viewer and in MPR.
- **MPR:** three planes with axial on top by default; link or unlink plane rotation; reset button; defaults in Settings › MPR.
- **3D:** heart isolation for cardiac CTA (automatic and undoable), the CT table and mattress removed, lungs kept in small fields of view, and icons for Cut (long-press for names).
- **Study quality:** slice spacing and gaps, resolution, compression, dose settings, and measured noise, with verdicts for MPR and 3D.
- **Home screen** swipes between Studies, Albums, and Transfers. New app icon, clearer empty screens, progress bars, and plainer error messages.

### Install
1. Download **InsulaDICOMViewer-v1.9.0.apk** below on your Android phone.
2. Open it, and allow installing from this source when Android asks.
3. Already using Insula? Install over it (or use **About › Check for updates**); your studies and settings are kept.
