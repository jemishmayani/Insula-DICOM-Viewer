Free, open-source DICOM viewer for Android 7.0 and newer. Your images stay on your phone.

> **Not a medical device.** For reference, teaching, and patient use, not primary diagnosis. See [Intended use and limitations](https://github.com/jemishmayani/Insula-DICOM-Viewer/blob/main/docs/INTENDED_USE.md).

### What's new in 1.9.1
- **Faster scrolling:** slices you scroll past are no longer decoded one by one before the one you stop on, so the image appears right away even after a quick scroll.
- **3D: the CT table and cradles are removed**, including curved head and body cradles and tables touching the skin, while the body and fine internal vessels are kept. If you saved a 3D session for a study earlier, choose **Start fresh** when the 3D view opens to apply the new separation.

### Install
1. Download **InsulaDICOMViewer-v1.9.1.apk** below on your Android phone.
2. Open it, and allow installing from this source when Android asks.
3. Already using Insula? Install over it (or use **About › Check for updates**); your studies and settings are kept.
