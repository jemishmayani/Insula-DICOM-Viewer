Free, open-source DICOM viewer for Android 7.0 and newer. Your images stay on your phone.

> **Not a medical device.** For reference, teaching, and patient use, not primary diagnosis. See [Intended use and limitations](https://github.com/jemishmayani/Insula-DICOM-Viewer/blob/main/docs/INTENDED_USE.md).

### What's new in 1.7.0
- **Smoother scrolling:** slices load in the background, so the screen never pauses, and the app reads ahead in the direction you scroll.
- **Snappier window/level and MPR:** redraws once per frame across CPU cores; MPR opens faster and stays responsive while you drag.
- **New gestures:** flick to glide through a series, drag the right edge to jump anywhere, long-press for window presets.
- **Quick bar:** Scroll, Window, Measure, Presets, Play, Compare, Layout, Link, Align, Reset, and More, one tap each.
- **Compare with prior studies:** side by side, with the best-matching series and scrolling linked across studies (**Align** fine-tunes it).
- **Remembers your window settings** for each series.
- **First use:** a welcome screen and a synthetic demo study (a prior and a current CT head, no real patient) to try every tool.
- **Tidier Settings and Guide:** the Settings header shows version and library details; the Guide has collapsible sections and search.
- **Android 16 ready:** targets API level 36 with edge-to-edge layout and predictive back, and still runs on Android 7.0 and later.

### Install
1. Download **InsulaDICOMViewer-v1.7.0.apk** below on your Android phone.
2. Open it, and allow installing from this source when Android asks.
3. Already using Insula? Install over it (or use **About › Check for updates**); your studies and settings are kept.
