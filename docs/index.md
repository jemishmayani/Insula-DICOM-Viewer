# Insula DICOM Viewer

A free, open-source DICOM viewer for Android: open studies from files, patient CDs, and hospital PACS; measure and annotate; reconstruct in MPR and 3D; and share anonymized copies. Images stay on your phone.

> Not a medical device. For reference, teaching, and patient use, not primary diagnosis.
