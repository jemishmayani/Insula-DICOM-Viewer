Free, open-source DICOM viewer for Android 7.0 and newer. Your images stay on your phone.

> **Not a medical device.** For reference, teaching, and patient use, not primary diagnosis. See [Intended use and limitations](https://github.com/jemishmayani/Insula-DICOM-Viewer/blob/main/docs/INTENDED_USE.md).

### What's new in 1.6.0
- **About page:** Settings › About Insula, or tap the card at the top of Settings. Version, disclaimer, links to the guide, source code, releases, issues, and privacy policy, the licence texts, and developer contact.
- **Check for updates:** asks GitHub for the newest release, only when you tap it.
- **Licence texts in the app:** the GNU GPL, the NOTICE, and the JJ2000 licence.
- **Copy app and device info** for bug reports. *Report a problem* reminds you never to attach patient images.

### Also in 1.5.x
JPEG 2000 support, PACS downloads up to 30× faster, measurement tools next to the pencil, a loop-speed slider (1–60 fps), a redesigned Settings screen, and the GPL-3.0 licence. See the [changelog](https://github.com/jemishmayani/Insula-DICOM-Viewer/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

### Install
1. Download **InsulaDICOMViewer-v1.6.0.apk** below on your Android phone.
2. Open it, and allow installing from this source when Android asks.
3. Already using Insula? Install over it; your studies and settings are kept.
