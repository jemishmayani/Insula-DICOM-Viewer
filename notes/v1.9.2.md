Free, open-source DICOM viewer for Android 7.0 and newer. Your images stay on your phone.

> **Not a medical device.** For reference, teaching, and patient use, not primary diagnosis. See [Intended use and limitations](https://github.com/jemishmayani/Insula-DICOM-Viewer/blob/main/docs/INTENDED_USE.md).

### What's new in 1.9.2
- **New app icon:** stacked image slices with an axial brain. Your launcher can shape it (circle, squircle, rounded square) without clipping, and on Android 13+ it follows your themed-icon colours.
- **3D VRT is now labelled Beta.** Automatic tissue separation is rule-based and won't always be right; a one-time notice explains how to correct results with Tissues, Pick, Cut, and Clip, and how to report problems. 3D views are for orientation and communication, not diagnosis.

### Install
1. Download **InsulaDICOMViewer-v1.9.2.apk** below on your Android phone.
2. Open it, and allow installing from this source when Android asks.
3. Already using Insula? Install over it (or use **About › Check for updates**); your studies and settings are kept. The new icon may take a moment to appear while your launcher refreshes.
